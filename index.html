04 / About

A bit
about me.

I’ve spent about a decade working in data science and machine learning across real estate, cybersecurity, and ad-tech, at startups and larger companies. I’m now part of the founding team at Accomplish, working on AI agents and how they learn.

Earlier, I was part of the founding team at Skyline AI, which was acquired by JLL in 2021.

I have a BSc and MSc in Computer Science from Bar-Ilan University. My studies focused on machine learning and network security.

I also have two patent applications from my time at JLL, covering novel entity resolution using generative AI and domain-specific recommender systems. Entity resolution means matching records that refer to the same real-world entity. The recommender work accounts for geospatial context, company hierarchies, and other domain signals.

I’m married to Almog, and we have three children who are getting into AI too. They’ve even been inventing their own games. I enjoy learning new things and helping others learn, too.
